Politique en matière de cookies

Dernière mise à jour : 22 novembre 2026

Ces documents juridiques sont fournis en anglais, qui fait foi.

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Eco Tax Pay uses essential cookies, which are needed for the site to work and do not require consent. Our live chat (tawk.to) sets its own cookies, so it is only loaded if you choose Accept in the cookie banner. We use no analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

Cookies we set

NamePurposeDuration
etp_sessionKeeps a host logged in. Signed and HTTP-only, so it cannot be read by scripts or forged.30 days, or until you log out
langRemembers the language you chose in the 🌐 menu (or the language a host set in your link).1 year
cookie_consentRemembers your choice in the cookie banner ("Accept" or "Essential only").1 year
tzYour device's time zone (e.g. Europe/Malta), so prices and examples are shown in your local currency. Not used to track you.1 year

Third-party cookies

When a host pays their subscription, the card payment takes place on Stripe's secure checkout page, and Stripe sets its own essential cookies there to prevent fraud. Guests pay their host directly, through the host's own bank or payment app. These are governed by Stripe's cookie policy.

Our live chat is provided by tawk.to and loads only after you accept in the cookie banner. It then sets its own cookies (such as __tawkuuid and TawkConnectionTime) so a conversation continues as you move between pages. See tawk.to's privacy policy.

Managing cookies

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer of every page. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings; if you block essential cookies, logging in and remembering your language will not work.

More about how we handle data: Privacy Policy. Questions: support@ecotaxpay.com.
