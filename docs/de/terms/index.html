These terms apply to the Eco Tax Pay website and service (the "Service"), operated by Eco Tax Pay ("we", "us"). By creating an account or paying through a Eco Tax Pay link you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. What the Service does

Eco Tax Pay lets accommodation providers ("Hosts") calculate local tourist, eco or visitor taxes, send their guests ("Guests") a payment link showing the amount and the Host's own payment details, issue receipts once the Host confirms payment, optionally collect identity documents, import bookings from the Host's calendars, and share an arrival guide after payment.

We provide software only. We are not a tax authority, tax adviser or law firm, and we are not the collector of any tax. Each Host remains solely responsible for registering with, declaring to and paying over tax to the relevant authority, and for complying with local law.

2. Our role: a platform only, we never handle funds

Eco Tax Pay is a software platform that helps Hosts calculate and request the tourist or eco tax from their Guests. It only facilitates that process. Specifically:

  • We never receive, hold, transfer or have access to any money paid by Guests, and we never take any part of the tax. Guests pay the Host directly, using payment details the Host chooses and controls (the Host's own bank account, payment app or payment provider).
  • Any payment is a matter solely between the Host and the Guest. We are not a party to it, and we are not a bank, payment institution, payment service provider, money transmitter, escrow agent or the Host's agent for collecting money.
  • We do not check, guarantee or confirm that a Guest has paid. A payment is marked as received, and a receipt issued, only when the Host confirms it.
  • The Host alone is responsible for the payment details they publish, for receiving and checking payments, for refunds, failed, late, missing or duplicate payments, chargebacks and disputes with Guests, and for declaring and paying the tax over to the relevant authority. Guests should contact the Host about any payment matter.
  • The only payment we process is the Host's own Eco Tax Pay subscription, which is handled by Stripe (see section 5).

3. Tax rates

Built-in rates are researched from public sources and dated on the Destinations & rates page, but rates and rules change and sources sometimes disagree. Hosts must check that the rate, exemptions and caps applied to each stay are correct before sending a link. Rates entered by a Host ("Other destination") are the Host's responsibility. We are not liable for tax under- or over-charged because of an incorrect rate.

4. Host accounts

  • You must be at least 18 and provide accurate details. You are responsible for everything done with your account and for keeping your password secret.
  • You may only add accommodation you are entitled to let, and only send links to your own guests.
  • You must have a lawful basis for any guest data you ask for, including identity documents, and tell guests why you collect them.
  • Calendar links you connect (for example Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Misterb&b or a channel manager) are used only to show your upcoming bookings to you. Booking platforms do not share guest names through these links.
  • The payment details you add (bank account, Revolut, PayPal, card payment link and similar) are shown to the Guests you send links to. You are responsible for keeping them correct.

5. Subscription, billing and cancellation

  • The subscription starts when you create your account and complete payment on Stripe's secure checkout page. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your full card details.
  • Hosts pay a subscription of €3 per month, or the equivalent in their local currency at the European Central Bank reference rate on the day they subscribe (the exact amount is shown before payment and stays the same for that subscription), charged automatically to the payment method on file at the start of each monthly period until cancelled. Stripe emails a receipt for each payment, and invoices are available under Account → Payment method and invoices. Prices include or exclude VAT as shown at checkout.
  • You can cancel at any time from Account → Cancel subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month: you will not be charged again and you keep access until then. We do not refund partial months, except where the law requires it.
  • If a payment fails we may retry it, and we may suspend the creation of new payment links until it succeeds.
  • We may change the price with at least 30 days' notice by email. If you do not agree, you can cancel before the change applies.
  • Consumers in the EU/UK may have a 14-day right of withdrawal from the subscription; by starting to use the Service straight away you ask us to begin immediately, and a withdrawal within 14 days is refunded pro rata for the time used.

6. Guest payments

Guests pay the Host directly before arrival, using the payment methods the Host lists (for example a card payment link, bank transfer, Revolut, PayPal, Wise, Bizum or Wero). As set out in section 2, we never receive, hold or transfer Guests' money, and we are not a party to the payment between the Host and the Guest. A receipt is issued only when the Host confirms the payment has arrived. The amount is set by the Host's link and cannot be changed by the Guest. Questions about the amount, payment, refunds (for example after a cancelled or shortened stay) and the stay itself should go to the Host, who is responsible for them. Receipts are issued in the Host's name and are not government documents.
