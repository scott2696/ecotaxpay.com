For holiday rentals, B&Bs and hotels

Tourist tax, paidbefore guests arrive.

Send your guests a single payment link. They pay the exact tourist tax online before check-in, and you receive the funds and a receipt for every stay. No cash on arrival. €3 per month.

  • ✓Accurate local rates for 13 countries, calculated automatically, or set your own anywhere in the world
  • ✓Guests pay you directly before they arrive: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Revolut, PayPal, Wise, Bizum, MB WAY, Satispay, TWINT, Wero, bank transfer and more
  • ✓Automatic receipts, optional passport or ID copy and a private arrival guide

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?
From €3 a month: €3 monthly, €8 for 3 months, €16 for 6 months or €30 for a year. Billed automatically to your card; unlimited properties and payment links.
Can I send a payment link without setting anything up?
Yes. Choose Quick link, enter the amount and send the link to your guest. No property, calendar or stay details are needed. Your guest pays you directly, and once you confirm the payment they receive a receipt and it appears on your dashboard.
Who pays the tourist tax?
Your guests pay the tax to you directly before they arrive, using your own payment details, and you then pay it to your local tax authority as usual. The money never passes through Eco Tax Pay, and we never take any portion of the tax.
How do guests pay?
With the methods you choose: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay or Google Pay through your own card payment link (SumUp, Square, Stripe…), bank transfer, Revolut, PayPal, Wise, Bizum, MB WAY, Satispay, TWINT or Wero. Guests see your details and a payment reference, pay you, and tap “I've paid”; you confirm and the receipt is sent.
Which destinations are supported?
Malta, Barcelona, the Balearic Islands, Lisbon, Porto, Rome, Greece, Amsterdam, Berlin, Vienna, Prague, Dubrovnik, Ljubljana, Edinburgh and Oʻahu (Hawaii) are built in. For any other location, you can enter the local rate yourself.
Which booking platforms can I connect?
Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Misterb&b, Expedia, Agoda, Holidu, Tripadvisor and more, plus channel managers such as Smoobu, Lodgify, Beds24, Hostaway and Guesty, Google Calendar, or any platform with an iCal link. Upcoming bookings then appear on your dashboard, ready for a payment link.
How do I cancel my subscription?
  1. Log in and open Account (top right).
  2. Under Subscription, click Cancel subscription and confirm.
  3. Your subscription is now cancelled and you will not be charged again. The date your access ends is shown on the same page.
  • You keep full access until the end of your current billing period. Unused time is not refunded.
  • To reverse the cancellation, click Keep my subscription on the same page before that date.
  • Guests can still pay any links you have already sent, and you can continue to log in to view payments and download receipts and exports.
  • To resubscribe later, click Subscribe on the Account page. Your properties and arrival guides will be kept.
Is there a contract?
No. Your subscription renews for the period you chose (1, 3, 6 or 12 months), and you can cancel at any time in two clicks.
