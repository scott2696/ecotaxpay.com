Frequently asked questions

How is the Municipal Tourist Tax (Taxa Turística) calculated?

In Porto, the law requires every guest aged 13 or over to pay the Municipal Tourist Tax (Taxa Turística): €3.00 per person per night, capped at 7 nights per stay. Children under 13 are exempt. Your host collects this tax and pays it to the local authority.

Do children pay?

Guests under 13 are exempt.

Is there a maximum?

Yes: capped at 7 nights per stay.

How do hosts collect the Municipal Tourist Tax (Taxa Turística) in Porto?

With Eco Tax Pay you send each guest a personal link that shows the Municipal Tourist Tax (Taxa Turística) with the legal explanation, in their language. The guest pays you directly (card link, bank transfer, Revolut and more) and gets a numbered receipt. You then pay the tax over to the authority as required.
